# Contact and workflow research (Milestone 007)

Open a saved opportunity → **Research contact and workflow**. Follow official contact/about/team links manually; the app retrieves only the single URL explicitly requested. Confirm public access and permission first. Existing SSRF/DNS pinning, robots, redirects, restricted-platform, content-type, size, timeout and website-restriction checks apply unchanged. Blocked/oversized pages remain manual research; concise manual excerpts are labelled operator-recorded, never server-verified.

Sources are appended separately from discovery/operator evidence, up to eight snapshots per opportunity. Select a snapshot, record an actually published name/role and route with verbatim evidence, observed workflow quote, a separate possible automation question and (only with real evidence and operator confirmation) an actual business problem. No names/emails are generated. Source and claim context remain the operator's responsibility: exact text matching cannot establish the truth or meaning of a quote. General business/B2B route purpose requires explicit source support and operator review; a public customer phone number alone does not establish permission for sales outreach. Customer booking forms/customer-only routes never qualify.

**Prepare researched approach with Qwen** uses the existing loopback Qwen3-4B adapter and exact-quote validator. It shows a source-based summary, route status, cautious angle and one short editable internal message. It never invents missing needs, budgets or consent. AI downtime produces an error, preserving research and manual editing. The model can select only supplied grounded evidence; deterministic message rendering keeps claims bounded. Automation questions are operator hypotheses, not confirmed pain. No sending exists.

**Save reviewed message to CRM drafts** requires explicit operator approval and a reviewed appropriate business email route. It uses the existing lead linking/deduplication and email-draft writer, preserves existing leads/drafts and does not infer an operational problem from a workflow fact. Customer-only/unknown routes cannot use this action. Phone-only research remains internal; the existing draft channels are email/LinkedIn. Once an opportunity has a linked lead, later research does not overwrite that CRM record: review changes there manually. Existing manual CRM/draft tools remain available and do not imply consent.

One nullable `opportunities.contact_research` JSONB column is added by migration `0005`; prior records remain compatible. Source snapshots retain original URL, origin and recording time. Annotating a snapshot cannot replace its server-stored text. Row locks preserve concurrent source edits. Changing the opportunity's company identity or primary source URL clears this research to prevent cross-company evidence reuse. Snapshot dates are visible; operator must recheck relevance/current role and route before any approach. No geographic/source expansion or automated crawl.

## Live evaluation (28 October 2026, Pakistan)

The three requested Raleigh/Apex cohort businesses were reviewed. Five pages were successfully retrieved through the app safeguards; two pages exceeded the unchanged size limit and were recorded via the manual research path. Two published leadership associations were identified, three public customer/business phone routes were found, and **zero explicitly supported B2B/sales email routes, zero verified operational problems and zero buying requests** were established. All three remain **Contact research needed**.

Real Qwen generation succeeded for all three: three short grounded internal messages, each with uncertainty. They are conversation hypotheses for manual review, not approved/sent outreach or evidence of buyer demand. No real CRM lead/draft was created by the research run. Full named findings, original URLs and messages remain in ignored `.local-research/contact-research-report.md` and saved opportunity research, not Git. Source page publication does not prove delivery, consent or unmet need. Commercial usefulness is limited until an appropriate route and a real priority are confirmed.

Tests cover guessed contacts/quotes, customer booking exclusion, unknown purpose, source origins, grounded/ungrounded model responses, model unavailable, Neon source/annotation persistence, explicit approval, CRM handoff without fabricated problem/score, and draft preservation. Existing retrieval/CRM/CSV/draft regressions run alongside these. Local research/runtime files are excluded from Git, lint and TypeScript compilation; synthetic fixtures remain tested.
